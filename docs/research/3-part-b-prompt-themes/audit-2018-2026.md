# CEED Part B Prompt Themes — Coded Audit 2018–2026

Scope: every Part B question in the official CEED question papers 2018–2026 (51 questions: 41 mandatory + 10 optional specialisation questions in 2018–2019). All prompts read from the official PDFs; image-only prompts (2025 Q2, Q4) rendered to images and read directly. No coaching transcriptions used.

Coding legend: **USER** = who the prompt is about/for · **SET** = setting · **OBJ** = artefact type produced · **TASK** = core verb · **POV** = viewpoint constraint · **MED** = medium constraint · **TONE** = tone asked or implied · **HOOK** = context hook · **CRIT** = stated evaluation criteria. "—" = not present.

## 1. Coded question-by-question table (what was actually asked?)

### Takeaway
Two eras. 2018–2019: 8 questions, choose 1 of 5 specialisation design briefs (50 or 40 marks) + 3 mandatory (Sketching, Creativity, Visualization). 2020–2026: 5 mandatory x 20 marks in fixed slots (Sketching, Creativity, Visual Sensitivity, Form Sensitivity, Problem Identification). Every question states its own evaluation criteria.

- Creativity-slot stimuli in 2019, 2021, 2022, 2023 were printed in the answer booklet, so the exact elements are mostly unrecoverable from the QP (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- Exact visual elements for Creativity 2019, 2021 (incl. the 3 printed themes), 2022, 2023 (incl. given first words), and the 2024 Q1 frame layout appear only in answer booklets, not in the published QPs. Not recovered; no coaching fallback used.
- Images for 2021 Q4 bottle, 2023 Q5 bus stop, 2024 Q5 rural kitchen, 2025 Q5 cart, 2022 Q5 train were not described in detail here beyond the prompt text (all present in the PDFs).
- 2022 Q4 building and 2025 Q4 product identifications are mine from the images; the papers carry no attribution.

## 2. Frequency counts, recurring themes, one-offs

### Takeaway
Across the 41 mandatory questions, children are the dominant protagonist (8 questions), home/kitchen the dominant setting (~10), and Indian everyday context the dominant hook (~14). Humour/surprise has been an explicit criterion every year since 2022. COVID-19 is the only clearly year-specific current event in mandatory questions (3 questions, 2021–2022); the 2019 Cricket World Cup appears in an optional brief.

## 3. Format evolution 2018–2026

### Takeaway
2018–19 tested specialisation (choose 1 of 5 design disciplines, 40–50 marks) plus generic drawing skills; from 2020 the specialisation briefs were dropped and Part B became 5 equal-weight generic aptitude questions, adding Form Sensitivity and Problem Identification. Time stayed 2 hours, total 100 marks throughout.

### Cited Findings
| Year | Structure | Slots (marks) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2018 | 8 Qs; answer 1 of Q1–5 + Q6–8 mandatory | Industrial / Communication / Animation / Interaction / Mobility (choose 1, 50); Sketching 20; Creativity 20; Visualization 10 | [QP2018](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED2018qp.pdf) |
| 2019 | Same, re-weighted | Specialisation (choose 1, 40, internally split 10/10/20 or 10x4 or 30+10); Sketching 20; Creativity 20; Visualization 20 | [QP2019](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED2019qp.pdf) |
| 2020 | "All questions are mandatory"; 5 x 20 | Sketching; Creativity; Visual Sensitivity; Form; Problem identification | [QP2020](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED2020qp.pdf) |
| 2021–2024 | 5 x 20 mandatory | Same order; "Form" renamed "Form sensitivity" from 2021 | [QP2021](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED_2021_Question_Paper.pdf), [QP2024](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED_2024_Question_Paper.pdf) |
| 2025–2026 | 5 x 20 mandatory; each labelled "Mandatory question" | Same order; 2025 sub-marks stated as 4x5 for most | [QP2025](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED_2025_Question_Paper.pdf), [QP2026](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED_2026_Question_Paper.pdf) |

- Part B duration 2 hours, starting after 1-hour Part A, in all years read. — [QP2018](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED2018qp.pdf), [QP2026](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED_2026_Question_Paper.pdf)
- Colour pencils, crayons, sketch pens allowed unless a question restricts. — [QP2018](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED2018qp.pdf), [QP2020](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED2020qp.pdf)
- 2019 Animation note: the frame image "did not appear on screen during the examination," so any frame proportion was accepted — evidence questions are shown on screen. — [QP2019](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED2019qp.pdf)
- Lineage: 2018–19 "Visualization" (lettering, motif) → 2020+ "Visual Sensitivity" (icons, and lettering again 2024). 2019 Industrial Design's "list ten critical factors" and 2018 Mobility's "identify five factors with sketches" foreshadow the 2020+ Problem Identification slot. — [QP2018](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED2018qp.pdf), [QP2019](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED2019qp.pdf)
- Problem ID drift: no solutions (2020, 2022 explicit) → list + illustrate (2021–2025) → two stakeholders (2025) → HMW framing + 2 drawn solutions worth 12/20 (2026). — [QP2020](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED2020qp.pdf), [QP2022](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED_2022_Qestion_Paper.pdf), [QP2025](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED_2025_Question_Paper.pdf), [QP2026](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED_2026_Question_Paper.pdf)
- Sketching drift: room/object perspective (2018–19) → character consistency without environment (2020–22) → figure group from odd POV (2023) → animation-principle sequence (2024) → full scene with environment from non-human POV (2025–26). — sources as §1
- Creativity drift: object recombination (2018) → complete abstract marks + title (2019–23; humour criterion from 2022) → premise transformation (2024) → narrative sequence from fixed icons (2025–26). — sources as §1
- Form drift: match a given product part (2020–21) → borrow language from architecture/sculpture/character product (2022, 2023, 2025) → primitive-to-furniture (2024, 2026). — sources as §1
- Criteria count shrinks from 5–7 long-form (2018–19) to a stable 4 per question by 2025–26. — [QP2018](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED2018qp.pdf), [QP2026](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED_2026_Question_Paper.pdf)

### Inferences
- The 2020 switch likely aimed at a discipline-neutral aptitude test (all candidates face identical questions, easier to compare). Not stated officially.
- 2026 PI now rewards solution ideation; practice should train both "frame problems" and "sketch 2 solutions fast" within ~24 min.
- Sketching has shifted from technical perspective to storytelling scenes; character expression and environment are now both scored.

### Gaps
- No official statement explaining the 2020 format change.
- Whether 2020 transition was announced in that year's brochure was not checked (brochures not in scope).

## 5. Prompt-generation grammar for practice Part B prompts

### Takeaway
Each 2020+ slot reduces to a template with 4–6 variable slots plus one "twist rule". The value lists below are drawn from the coded papers (marked ✓ where a value actually appeared) and extended with same-register values (unmarked). Criteria are fixed per slot and mirror 2025–26 wording.

### Cited Findings
All ✓ values trace to §1 (sources [QP2018](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED2018qp.pdf)–[QP2026](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED_2026_Question_Paper.pdf)). Unmarked values are my extrapolations, not sourced.

**Global slots (apply to all question types)**
- `MARKS` = 20 (internal split 4x5, 10+10, 5+3x5, 2x4+6x2)
- `TIME_BUDGET` ≈ 24 min (inferred: 120 min / 5)
- `MEDIUM` ∈ {black/HB pencil only ✓, pencils + no colour ✓, BLACK pen/pencil only ✓, B&W only ✓, no text ✓, no drawing instruments ✓, draw only within boxes ✓, colours allowed ✓}
- `INDIAN_ANCHOR` (named person/place, optional) ∈ {Chinnu/Chinu ✓, Rohan ✓, Gouri from Versova ✓, Mr. Singh's family ✓, Kaa & Kii ✓, Srinagar→Chennai ✓, mela ✓ …}

**Q1 Sketching**
```
[CHARACTERS] are [ACTION] in [SETTING] [TIME/MOMENT]. [INCITING EVENT].
Sketch the scene from the point of view of [POV].
Rules: [MEDIUM]; [ENVIRONMENT RULE]; [FRAME RULE].
Criteria: character & expressions · proportion & composition · quality of sketching · environment & props
```
- `CHARACTERS` ∈ {child + pet ✓, two kids ✓, family of 4 w/ heights ✓, fisherwoman ✓, health worker + patient ✓, man with object ✓, grandparent + grandchild, street vendor + customer, cricket-playing kids}
- `SETTING` ∈ {kitchen ✓, room w/ dims ✓, forest ✓, pavement ✓, clinic ✓, mango orchard ✓, bus stop, railway platform, terrace on kite day, classroom}
- `INCITING EVENT` ∈ {parent enters ✓, animal chases ✓, selfie taken ✓, object broken ✓, swab inserted ✓, rain starts, power cut, monkey steals food}
- `POV` ∈ {chosen by candidate ✓, standing adult 5½ ft ✓, ceiling-corner camera ✓, fox (hidden animal) ✓, chair (inanimate) ✓, ceiling fan, pet at floor level, CCTV, a ball mid-air, inside a fridge}

- `FRAME RULE` ∈ {single scene ✓, 2 separate situations of a given character ✓, 4-frame sequence w/ anticipation & exaggeration ✓}

```

```

**Q3 Visual Sensitivity**

Type D (lettering): Given letters [X, Y] of a [STYLE] font, write [WORD] / draw [LETTER] at [SIZE].
Type E (identity): Visual identity for [ORGANISATION] incorporating [ABBREVIATION].

- Generator rule for realism: pick one slot template, fill from ✓ values with ~70% probability and extension values ~30%, add Indian anchor for Sketching and PI, and apply at most one TWIST per full mock paper.
- Weighting by recency: 2025–26 wording (4 criteria, "Mandatory question," environment drawn in Sketching, HMW in PI) is the best predictor for 2027.
- 2018–19 optional briefs (vehicle, app, storyboard, book cover) are not part of the current exam but are good stretch exercises for portfolio/interview stages.
