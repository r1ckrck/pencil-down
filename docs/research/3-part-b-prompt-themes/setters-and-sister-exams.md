# CEED Paper Setters and Sister-Exam Themes

Evidence labels: **[V]** verified from an official IIT Bombay or IDC page. **[C]** coaching or aggregator source, often memory-based, so treat it as unverified. **[I]** my inference.

## 1. Who organises and sets CEED?

### Takeaway
IIT Bombay runs CEED through a joint "UCEED-CEED Office" headed by a Chairperson. No official page I found says who writes the questions. The claim that IDC faculty set Part B is a strong inference, not a published fact.

### Cited Findings
- [V] CEED 2027 is conducted by IIT Bombay. The contact is "Chairperson, UCEED-CEED Office, Indian Institute of Technology Bombay". The page names no organising committee and no paper-setting body. Exam date: 17 Jan 2027, 9:00–12:00. — [CEED 2027 site](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/)
- [V] The only IDC credit on the CEED site is "Designed by IDC School of Design" (the website itself). IDC is also listed as IIT Bombay's participating unit. — [CEED 2027 site](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/)
- [V] UCEED uses the same office: "Chairperson, UCEED-CEED Office, IIT Bombay", contact uceed@iitb.ac.in. — [UCEED 2027 site](https://www.uceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/)
- [V] Part B has five questions that test "design, drawing and writing skills". The questions appear on screen and answers go into a physical answer book. — [CEED 2027 site](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/)
- [V] The official site lists previous question papers and answer keys for 2020–2026 under its downloads folder. — [CEED 2027 site](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/)
- [C] Wikipedia says CEED is "hosted by the Industrial Design Centre, Indian Institute of Technology Bombay" on behalf of the Ministry of Human Resource Development. It gives no history of the host changing. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Entrance_Examination_for_Design)
- [C] Older brochures describe CEED as conducted by IIT Bombay on behalf of the Ministry of Human Resource Development (MHRD). — [CEED 2019 brochure via EntranceZone](https://www.entrancezone.com/admissions/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/CEED-2019-Information-Brochure.pdf)

### Inferences
- [I] Unlike GATE and JEE Advanced, whose organising IIT rotates each year, CEED has always stayed at IIT Bombay. So the same faculty pool, IDC's, is the most plausible source of questions year after year. That would explain the stable five-category Part B format (sketching, creativity, form, visual, problem identification).
- [I] The Chairperson of the UCEED-CEED Office is an IIT Bombay faculty appointment. Its name is not published on the exam sites.

### Gaps
- I found no official statement, interview or FAQ describing how the Part B paper is set or moderated, or which institutes' faculty take part.
- I found no list of past Chairpersons and no evidence that the chair rotates.

## 3. Do Part B categories mirror IDC disciplines? Any commentary?

### Takeaway
The structural mirror is strong. Icons map to communication design, POV scenes and storyboards to animation, primitive-form furniture to industrial design, and problem identification to interaction design. I found no mentor or topper commentary that explicitly says "the paper reflects IDC's interests".

### Cited Findings
- [C] One coaching analysis of the CEED 2026 paper lists Part B as Sketching, Creativity, Form Sensitivity, Visual Sensitivity and Problem Identification: five questions, 20 marks each. — [Toprankers via search summary](https://www.toprankers.com/news/current-affairs/ceed-2026-question-paper) (the page later returned 404)
- [C] CEED 2025 Part B reportedly asked for four icons, in black only, showing which animal is in each zoo enclosure. CEED 2021 had a hospital-ward icon set with the instruction that "all icons should follow the same visual language". — [CEED 2025 paper, Collegedunia copy](https://assets.collegedunia.com/public/image/CEED_2025_Question_Paper_444e638e7e8fcb9e53d54e9806c7901c.pdf); [CEED 2021 paper, Silica copy](https://silica.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/ceed-2021-original-paper.pdf) (both via search summary; the PDFs were not opened)
- [C] Careers360 reports that one CEED paper's Part B covered "character design, 2D graphics icon, problem identification on a toothbrush, and mixer grinder". The page labels this data 2023, so the year is ambiguous. — [Careers360 CEED analysis](https://design.careers360.com/articles/ceed-exam-analysis)
- [C] The final CEED score reportedly combines the two parts as 0.25 × Part A + 0.75 × Part B. — [Careers360 evaluation criteria](https://design.careers360.com/articles/ceed-2026-result-evaluation-criteria)

### Inferences
- [I] The prompt subjects repeat across years:
  - zoo icons (2025)
  - hospital-ward icons (2021)
  - toothbrush and mixer grinder (reported)
  - medication pictograms (an IDC student project)
  - portable hand-wash station (an IDC student project)
- [I] All of these are public-service or everyday-object design for Indian users, which is IDC's social and inclusive design ethos.

### Gaps
- I found no first-hand topper, mentor or IDC-faculty statement linking the paper's content to IDC's research interests.
- I could not open the official CEED 2024–2026 Part B PDFs; two aggregator pages returned 410 or 404. The official papers are on [ceed.iitb.ac.in](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/) and should be the primary source for exact wording.

## 4. Do the other participating institutes have a role in setting the paper?

### Takeaway
I found no evidence of one. Other institutes only use CEED scores for their own admissions and run their own shortlists and interviews.

### Cited Findings
- [V] CEED 2027 participating institutes: IIT Bombay (IDC), IIT Delhi, IIT Gandhinagar, IIT Guwahati, IIT Hyderabad, IIT Jodhpur, IIT Kanpur, IIT Roorkee, IISc Bangalore (CPDM), IIITDM Jabalpur, IIITDM Kancheepuram. — [CEED 2027 site](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/)
- [C] The UCEED-CEED Office at IIT Bombay does not decide the admission criteria, scholarships or financial assistance of individual institutes. — [search summary of CEED brochure sources](https://www.scribd.com/document/697067043/CEED2024-Brochure)

### Inferences
- [I] Participating institutes are score users only. Their programme mix (product, interaction, vehicle, UX) does not shape the paper beyond what IDC already covers.

### Gaps
- No public document confirms or rules out external faculty acting as paper setters or moderators.

## 5. Sister exams: recent prompts and overlap with CEED

### Takeaway
The UCEED Part B drawing prompts from 2020–2026 have the clearest overlap with CEED, and they are set by the same office. They follow 2 templates:

1. **Narrated Indian domestic or school scene, drawn from an unusual point of view** (dog, floor level, canteen owner).
2. **Compact portable product for a specific Indian user and setting** (child's lunch box, pocket seat, hostel toiletry kit).

NID and NIFT prompts are less reliably documented. Their reported themes are perspective and creature POV, sustainability or slow fashion, and "futuristic".

### Cited Findings: UCEED Part B, paraphrased by Careers360 from the official papers
| Year | Prompt | Template |
|---|---|---|
| 2026 | A boy walks his dog on a lamp-lined path in the evening; the dog turns back. "Draw this scene from the point of view of the dog." | Unusual POV |
| 2026 | Compact portable toiletry organizer for students using shared train and dorm bathrooms. Show it open for use and packed for carrying. | Portable product, Indian setting |
| 2025 | Lightweight, pocket-sized seat for museum or zoo visits, carried in a bag. Show it "just taken out of the bag". | Portable product, transformation |
| 2024 | School lunch box for a six-year-old's first day, holding typical Indian dry and liquid foods. Label its features. | Child user, Indian food |
| 2023 | A boy takes an online dance exam with his mother prompting him; the cat knocks over water, biscuits and books. Draw it from the floor. | Unusual POV, chaos, post-COVID online life |
| 2022 | A rat causes chaos in a school canteen. Draw it from the canteen owner's POV. | POV, school, chaos |
| 2021 | Drawing room at 9 a.m. with east sunlight, shadows, a cat, a newspaper, tea and potted plants. Draw it from your own POV. | Light and shadow, domestic |
| 2020 | Prof. Shastri's desk after a phone argument about a forgotten diet lunch. Draw it from her usual POV. | Narrative inferred from objects |

All rows — [Careers360 UCEED drawing questions](https://design.careers360.com/articles/uceed-drawing-questions). [V-adjacent] Official papers for 2020–2026 are listed at [UCEED downloads](https://www.uceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads.html), for example `assets/downloads/qps/UCEED_2026_Question_Paper.pdf`.

- [C] Sources conflict on UCEED Part B structure. Some describe a single 100-mark drawing question; others describe a drawing question plus a design-aptitude question (60 + 40). — [Careers360 UCEED 2025 analysis](https://design.careers360.com/articles/uceed-2025-exam-analysis)

### Cited Findings: NID DAT (unofficial; NID does not publish papers)
- [C] NID DAT 2025 (5 Jan 2025): the memory-based list includes "design a poster promoting orange juice from a crow's perspective" and a figure/posture-drawing question on "slow fashion and conscious buying". — [Careers360 NID DAT 2025 analysis](https://design.careers360.com/articles/nid-dat-exam-analysis); [Physics Wallah NID 2025 analysis](https://www.pw.live/design-architecture/exams/nid-exam-analysis-2025) (via search summary)
- [C] NID DAT 2026 Prelims (21 Dec 2025): one Careers360 page says the paper had no objective section and was all drawing and design thinking. Other sources say it mixed objective and subjective questions. Careers360 also says prelims carry no weight in final selection. — [Careers360 NID 2026 question paper](https://design.careers360.com/articles/nid-2026-question-paper); [Careers360 NID DAT 2026 analysis](https://design.careers360.com/articles/nid-dat-2026-exam-analysis)
- [C] I could not retrieve the specific 2026 NID subjective prompts; the Careers360 Part B page returned 410.

### Cited Findings: NIFT Situation Test
- [C] NIFT Situation Test 2025 (8 June 2025): a 2-hour offline test in which candidates build a 3D model from provided materials only, plus a write-up of about 100 words. The reported theme was "futuristic". Marking criteria: space visualisation, creative use of material, composition, colour, construction, presentation. — [Careers360 NIFT situation test 2025 analysis](https://design.careers360.com/articles/nift-situation-test-2025-analysis)

### Inferences
- [I] **Strongest cross-exam overlaps with CEED Part B**, ranked by weighting:
  1. **Unusual POV of a narrated scene.** This is UCEED's dominant template every year (animal, floor, bystander POV). NID uses the same idea with a crow's perspective.
  2. **Compact, portable or transforming products** for a named user in a named Indian setting (child, student, museum visitor, train or hostel). This matches CEED's form-sensitivity and problem-identification questions.
  3. **Icons and pictograms for a public-information system** (zoo enclosures, hospital wards). This also aligns with IDC's medication-pictogram work.
  4. **Everyday Indian domestic objects and settings**: tiffin and lunch box, canteen, chai, newspaper, mixer grinder, toothbrush, shared bathrooms.
  5. **Sustainability and conscious consumption** (NID slow fashion, IDC's LED-packaging reuse project).
- [I] **Users who recur across exams:** children and school students, hostel students, mothers and parents, professors or office workers, canteen staff, animals (dog, cat, rat, crow), museum and zoo visitors, patients.
- [I] **Current-events signal:** the online exam of 2023 (post-COVID online life) is the only clear topical hook. The prompts favour timeless everyday situations over headlines.

### Gaps
- Verified NID DAT and NIFT CAT prompts for 2022–2026 are not available from official sources. All recollections come from coaching sites, and several of those pages returned 404 or 410.
- I did not open the official UCEED or CEED PDFs; the UCEED wording above is Careers360's paraphrase.
- I have no data on NIFT CAT 2022–2026 prompts.
