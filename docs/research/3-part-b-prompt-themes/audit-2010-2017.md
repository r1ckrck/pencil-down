**How the text was extracted:**
- 2011 and 2012 are image-only scans. I ran OCR (optical character recognition, which turns scanned images into text) with tesseract. The OCR was clean enough to quote prompts.
- Every other year had a text layer and was extracted with pdftotext.
- Stimulus images were looked at directly for 2013 Q3 and 2014 Q2.
- No coaching-site transcriptions were needed.
- 2016 ran two sessions with different papers (marked 2016a and 2016b below).

## 1. Format by year (number of questions, marks, time)

### Takeaway
The format changed three times:
- **2010–2011:** one all-paper exam worth 100 marks. Five or six sections, each with a fixed theme.
- **2012–2014:** the exam split into Part A (objective questions) and Part B (pen-and-paper).
- **2013–2017:** Part B became "mandatory sketching, creativity and story questions plus ONE 50-mark design question chosen by discipline". The 2023+ pattern of 5 mandatory questions × 20 marks does not exist yet in this period.

### Inferences
- **Today's slots, traced back:**

  | Today's slot | Earlier form |
  |---|---|
  | Sketching | Drawing Abilities and Skills |
  | Creativity | Creativity I/II, magic device, alternate uses, word stories |
  | Problem Identification | Present 2010, 2011 and 2013 only, then absent 2014–2017 |
  | Visual Sensitivity, Form Sensitivity | Roughly the 2010–2011 "Visual Perception and Design Sensitivity" section, which was then dropped |

- The 50-mark discipline elective (2013–2017) is the biggest structural difference from today. Half of Part B was a mini design brief. A practice app covering 2010–2017 needs a "long brief" mode that today's 5 × 20 format lacks.

### Gaps
- Exam duration for 2010–2014 is not in the extracted text. It may be on cover pages that were not captured.
- 2012 OCR shows "CEED 2012 – B" in the footers. This may be a paper-set code; unconfirmed.

## 2. Coded question table (63 items)

**Code key**
- **USER:** who the design or scene is for or about.
- **SETTING:** where it takes place.
- **OBJECT:** the artefact type.
- **TASK:** the verb.
- **POV:** viewpoint constraint.
- **MEDIUM:** media or length constraint.
- **TONE:** humour, emotion, drama etc. if asked for.
- **HOOK:** the context it leans on.
- **EVAL:** stated evaluation criteria, abridged.

"—" means none stated. Each A/B/C option is coded as its own row.

### Takeaway
- **63 coded items:** 9 + 7 + 4 + 6 + 6 + 8 + 7 + 7 + 9 across 2010 to 2017, with 2016's two papers counted separately.
- **Fully written out.** Every prompt is text-described. Images only add to that, except 2014 Q2, where the image *is* the prompt.

### Takeaway

## 6. Evolution of format

### Takeaway
The papers moved in five steps:
1. **Many small sections, all on paper** (2010–11).
2. **Part A objective split** (2012).
3. **"Mandatory core + 50-mark discipline elective"** (2013–17).
4. **More mandatory creativity sub-questions with smaller marks** (2015–17).
5. **Evaluation criteria rewritten as "You will be evaluated for…" prose**, plus a free-colour rule (2016–17).

### Cited Findings
- **Discipline labels shifted:**

  | Year | Labels |
  |---|---|
  | 2010 | "Product Design / Interaction Design", "Communication Design", "Animation Design" |
  | 2014 | "Visual Communication and Interaction" (one combined elective) |
  | 2015–16 | Interaction Design split out as its own elective |
  | 2017 | Adds "Mobility and Vehicle Design"; Product becomes "Industrial Design" |

  — [2010](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED2010qp.pdf), [2014](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED2014qp.pdf), [2015](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED2015qp.pdf), [2017](https://www.ceed.iitb.ac.in/2027/downloads/CEED2017qp.pdf)
- **The scaffold (fixed answer structure) changed in 2014.** The 2010–2013 electives all used "≥5 factors → 3 concepts → final concept". From 2014 most briefs swap the factor list for deliverables: exploded view, ≥6 screens, ≥20 frames, model sheets, side elevation. — the papers above
- **Mark weights by year:**

  | Year | Drawing | Creativity | Elective |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 2010 | 20 | 10 + 10 (two questions) | 25 |
  | 2011 | 15 | 20 | 30 |
  | 2012 | 20 | — | 30 |
  | 2013–14 | 20 | 15 + 15 (two questions) | 50 |
  | 2015 | 20 | 10 + 12 + 8 (three questions) | 50 |
  | 2016 | 20 | 20 + 10 | 50 |
  | 2017 | 20 | 20 + 10 | 50 |

  — the papers above
- **Answer slots and word limits:**
  - 2010–2012 booklets printed answer slots.
  - From 2013, word limits appear: <20 words (2013), ≤250 words (2014), ≤50 and ≤100 words (2015).
  - **Sketching sometimes offers a choice of two prompts:** 2012, 2014 and 2017.
  - Problem identification disappears as a mandatory slot after 2013.

  — the papers above

### Inferences
- The 2017 structure (Sketching 20 + Creativity 20 + Stories 10 + elective 50) is the direct ancestor of today's 5 × 20 format. The elective's job (user-centred brief, interface, storyboard) was later spread across the Problem Identification, Visual Sensitivity and Form Sensitivity slots. This needs confirming against the 2018–2026 audit.
